OMS privacy policy

OMS (Operator Management System) is an internal tool of our customer support team. This page explains what happens when you sign in to it with Google.

What we receive from Google

Only your email address and the fact that Google has verified it (the "openid" and "email" permissions). OMS does not receive your password, contacts, files, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

What we use it for

  • To recognise you and show the dashboard that matches your role.
  • To keep you signed in for up to 30 days on the browser you used.
  • To record who opened OMS and who asked for access, in the team's internal log.

Sharing

Your email address is not sold or shared with anyone outside the support team's administrators, and is not used for advertising.

Removal

Ask the OMS administrator to remove your access at any time. You can also remove OMS from your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
